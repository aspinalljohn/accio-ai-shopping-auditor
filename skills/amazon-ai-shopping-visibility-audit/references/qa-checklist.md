# Completion QA

Run this check after drafting the report and before claiming completion.

## Identity and evidence

- [ ] Exact ASIN, marketplace, and visible variant are recorded.
- [ ] Conflicting variants, pack counts, dimensions, ingredients, or imagery are quarantined.
- [ ] Every material fact and factual rewrite has a source.
- [ ] Time-sensitive commerce data includes the capture date.
- [ ] Reviews and Q&A are labelled as shopper evidence, not manufacturer facts.

## Question set and scoring

- [ ] The report contains 20–30 product-specific buyer questions.
- [ ] At least five questions are high-intent and long-tail.
- [ ] Relevant taxonomy groups are represented without padding the set with irrelevant questions.
- [ ] Surface and question-level scores follow the 0–3 rubric.
- [ ] `N/A` values are excluded from calculations.
- [ ] Coverage math has been recalculated from the table.
- [ ] No metric is labelled an Amazon, Rufus, Alexa, or ranking score.

## Recommendations

- [ ] The 8–12 highest-value gaps are commercially prioritized.
- [ ] Content fixes are separated from fact-verification and operational fixes.
- [ ] Exact rewrites do not strengthen the underlying source claims.
- [ ] Unsupported or regulated claims remain blocked.
- [ ] Image callouts are limited to 1–3 strong expansion candidates unless more were requested.
- [ ] The report does not imply that recommendations were uploaded or published.

## Delivery

- [ ] Audit mode and access limitations are explicit.
- [ ] All referenced files exist.
- [ ] Output path is inside the active workspace, not the plugin directory.
- [ ] The required proprietary-system caveat is present verbatim.
- [ ] Completion summary names unresolved evidence gates.
