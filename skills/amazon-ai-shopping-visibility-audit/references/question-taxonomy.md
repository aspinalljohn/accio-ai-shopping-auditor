# Buyer-question taxonomy

Use this reference when generating the 20–30 questions for an audit. Questions must be specific to the product evidence; do not mechanically copy every example.

## Required coverage

Cover every relevant group and include at least five high-intent long-tail questions.

| Group | Buyer concern | Example pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Fit and user | Suitability for a person, environment, or use case | Is this good for [specific person/use case]? |
| Budget and value | Price ceiling, longevity, included quantity, alternatives | What is a good [product] under [price]? |
| Compatibility | Models, systems, spaces, ingredients, accessories | Will this work with [model/system/context]? |
| Material or ingredient | Composition, allergens, finishes, free-from concerns | What is it made from? |
| Size and capacity | Fit, dimensions, count, coverage, storage | Will this fit [space/body/item]? |
| Setup and care | Installation, assembly, cleaning, charging, storage | How hard is it to set up or maintain? |
| Durability and risk | Leaks, fading, breakage, rust, odor, wear | Will it hold up in [specific condition]? |
| Use-case specifics | A concrete job or situation | Can I use this for [scenario]? |
| Comparison | Meaningful differences from a close substitute | How is this different from [alternative]? |
| Objection handling | Common reasons not to buy or return | What should I know before buying? |
| Gifting and occasion | Recipient, packaging, event suitability | Is this a good gift for [person/occasion]? |
| Safety and compliance | Age, pet, skin, food contact, regulations | Is it safe for [context]? Only ask when relevant and source-backed. |

## High-intent patterns

Use combinations that resemble real buying decisions:

- good for [specific user/use case] under [price]?
- compatible with [specific model/context] and easy to [install/clean/store]?
- better for [priority] than [category alternative]?
- suitable for [edge condition] without [common failure]?
- worth the price for [specific usage frequency or outcome]?

Do not force price questions when no current price evidence exists. Label captured prices as time-sensitive.

## Priority heuristics

Prioritize questions that:

- change click or purchase confidence;
- expose common returns or negative-review themes;
- reveal missing specs or compatibility;
- distinguish the product from close substitutes;
- correspond to high-intent search behavior; and
- can be answered with source-backed product facts.

Avoid questions that require unsupported medical, legal, financial, safety, certification, or compliance claims.

## Category modifiers

Apply only the relevant modifiers:

- **Consumables and supplements:** serving count, ingredients, allergens, taste, storage, certifications, dietary fit, and claim substantiation.
- **Beauty and personal care:** ingredients, scent, texture, application, skin/hair type, finish, sensitivity, packaging, and routine compatibility.
- **Electronics:** device compatibility, power, ports, standards, setup, latency, battery, warranty, and included components.
- **Home improvement:** measurements, materials, installation type, tools, hardware, load/coverage, finish, indoor/outdoor use, and code-sensitive claims.
- **Apparel:** sizing, fit, material, care, opacity, stretch, season, body-type guidance, and variation consistency.
- **Pet and baby:** size/age fit, materials, cleaning, supervision, safety limitations, and compliance evidence.
- **Food and grocery:** count/weight, ingredients, allergens, preparation, storage, origin, flavor, dietary fit, and expiration handling.
